## Kiro v1.3.0 — Video editor and per-feature prices

- New in the Studio: a video editor. Import videos and photos from the gallery or files, reorder, trim, split, delete, change speed (0.5x–2x), fit to 9:16 (1080×1920) or 4:5, add text (Persian, right-to-left) and subtitles, background music (bundled CC0 tracks or your own file) with volume and automatic lowering under speech, on-device narration, simple transitions, fade in/out, colour presets and mute of the original sound.
- Free preview of the first 20 seconds; export to MP4 (H.264/AAC), save to the gallery and to drafts. Rendering runs in the foreground service and can be stopped.
- Prices in TON for every paid feature, shown before each action: topic video and own-scenes video by length (10 s 0.3 … 10 min 8), editor export (up to 1 min 0.2, up to 5 min 0.5, up to 10 min 1, longer 1.5), image 0.1, AI text (chat reply, caption and hashtags) 0.02.
- The first use of every paid feature is free on each device.
- Simpler payment: one "Pay with wallet" button that fills in the amount and comment; QR and comment are under "Manual payment". Payment ID and recovery code moved to Settings → Advanced / backup.
- Settings → Owner access: an owner code makes every paid feature free on that device.
- If credit cannot be opened, paid features show a friendly message; everything else, including model downloads, keeps working.
- The bundled FFmpeg can now read HEVC, MPEG-4, VP8/VP9, Opus and FLAC files, change audio speed and rotate phone videos correctly.

arm64-v8a only, Android 8.0+. No new network hosts. Until a fixed signing key is configured, a new build may not install over an older one; uninstall first, and restore your credit with the recovery code from Settings → Credit & recovery → Advanced / backup.
